D0P | D0N
D1P | D1N

D3P | D3N

Connection ExtBoard<->PMODs:
Do not plug a PMOD straight onto the ExtBoard. ExtBoard keeps each MDI pair
in a COLUMN (P/N stacked); the PMOD keeps each pair in a ROW (P | N). A
1:1 mate crosses pairs and only 10/100 (or nothing) comes up. Use jumpers.
ETH*_1 = MDI0 = PMOD D0, then 2→D1, 3→D2, 4→D3.
P goes to P, N to N. 3V3 only — leave 5V and NC unconnected.


PMOD ETH1 (top 6 rows of the ExtBoard)

ExtBoard     PMOD
-----------------
ETH1_1P  --> D0P
ETH1_1N  --> D0N
ETH1_2P  --> D1P
ETH1_2N  --> D1N
ETH1_3P  --> D2P
ETH1_3N  --> D2N
ETH1_4P  --> D3P
ETH1_4N  --> D3N
GND      --> GND   (both pins)
3V3      --> 3V3   (both pins)


PMOD ETH2 (bottom 6 rows of the ExtBoard)

ExtBoard     PMOD
-----------------
ETH2_1P  --> D0P
ETH2_1N  --> D0N
ETH2_2P  --> D1P
ETH2_2N  --> D1N
ETH2_3P  --> D2P
ETH2_3N  --> D2N
ETH2_4P  --> D3P
ETH2_4N  --> D3N
GND      --> GND   (both pins)
3V3      --> 3V3   (both pins)


If you stacked them without jumpers, the first row would be
ETH1_2P|ETH1_4P onto D0P|D0N — two different pairs, both positives.
That is the scramble. The middle 5V | GND | NC block is not a PMOD.
