Connection ExtBoard<->AD9248:
D0 = LSB .. D13 = MSB.
ACL/BCL are FPGA OUTPUTS (31.25 MHz, 180 deg). Module 5V on the 5V pin.

FPGA     ADC      role
-----------------------------
AB21 --> BOT      OTR_B (in)
U17  --> D1       data[1]
R17  --> D3       data[3]
R18  --> D5       data[5]
R19  --> D7       data[7]
N15  --> D9       data[9]
K14  --> D11      data[11]
M15  --> D13      data[13] MSB
N22  --> ACL      CLK_A (FPGA out)
J15  --> ------   NC
-----------------------------
Y21  --> BCL      CLK_B (FPGA out, ~CLK_A)
T18  --> D0       data[0] LSB
U18  --> D2       data[2]
N18  --> D4       data[4]
N19  --> D6       data[6]
M16  --> D8       data[8]
L15  --> D10      data[10]
L16  --> D12      data[12]
AA8  --> A0T      OTR_A (in)
J14  --> ------   NC

UDP on PHY0 only (one 1G cable):
  ch A port 5000,  ch B port 5001
14-bit packed offset-binary @ 31.25 MSPS/ch (~928 Mbps total).
